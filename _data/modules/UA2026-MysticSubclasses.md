## Features

- Full subclass integration in the FoundryVTT compendium system of the 4 Mystic Subclasses UA documents.
- Foundry use notes to aid in correctly using features.
- 3 Vestige Companion Stat blocks (official [Monster Manual](https://www.foundryvtt.store/products/dnd-monster-manual) module required for art to load.)

## Subclasses

- *Weave Martial and Mystic Arts* with the **Warrior of the Mystic Arts** (Monk)
- *Protect Your Allies from Villainous Magic* as an **Oath of the Spellguard** (Paladin)
- Slink around as the **Magic Stealer** (Rogue), *Stealing Magical Power to Empower Yourself*
- *Wield the Remnants of a Dying God’s Power* and command 3 Vestige Companions with a **Vestige Patron** (Warlock)
